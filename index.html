About CampusKit

Small tools for
big ambitions.

CampusKit UAE is a student entrepreneurship concept born at the University of Wollongong in Dubai. We believe getting organised should feel less like a chore and more like a fresh start.

This is a demonstration store created for an academic project.

Good to know

Questions,
answered.

Is this a real checkout?+

No — CampusKit is an entrepreneurship project demo. You can test the complete cart and checkout flow, but no payment is collected and no real order is placed.

Where do you deliver?+

This demo is designed around Dubai campus delivery. In a live version, delivery zones and timings would appear at checkout.

Can I buy individual products?+

Absolutely. Build your own study setup with individual pieces, or begin with a ready-curated kit.

What currency are prices shown in?+

All CampusKit prices are shown in UAE Dirham (AED), including the cart total.
